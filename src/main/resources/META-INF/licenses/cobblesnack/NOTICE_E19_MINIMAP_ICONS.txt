Bundled minimap-style Pokémon sprites
======================================

CobbleSnack includes the regular Pokémon sprites from:

E19 - Cobblemon Minimap Icons
Version: 1.21.1-1.4.4
Resource-pack author: Emery319
Original Cobblemon icons: MBCMechachu
Source: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/texture-packs/e19-cobblemon-minimap-icons

Only the regular sprite set is bundled. The sprite PNG files are unmodified and
retain their original paths under assets/xaerominimap/entity/icon/sprite/regular/.
